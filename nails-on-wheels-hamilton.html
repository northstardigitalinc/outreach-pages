The hands behind the clippers

Eight years of doing the one thing nobody wants to do.

Angeline trained at Gagemount Animal Hospital and built Nails On Wheels around a single idea: the nail trim is the part of grooming that stresses pets and owners the most, so bring it to the house and take the drive out of the equation.

Hamilton has voted her Best Pet Groomer at the Spectator Readers Choice Awards in 2022, 2023 and 2025, after taking Gold in 2020. Seventy-two reviews, every one of them a recommendation.

She is not a salon. She is one person with a van, a set of clippers, and eight years of hands that know how to hold a dog that does not want to be held.
